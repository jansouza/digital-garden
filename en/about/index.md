My name is Jan Souza and I've been working in technology for over twenty
years. I started out on telephony systems in 4GL/COBOL, spent years as a
middleware specialist putting out fires in critical environments, and
today I work as a Consulting Architect, helping teams adopt SRE practices:
reliability, observability and less repetitive manual work.

I've always been curious beyond what the job asked for. I like to
understand how things work on the inside, and that often takes me out of
scope: I built a Kubernetes cluster with Raspberry Pis just because I
could, 3D printed a rack to organize that same cluster and ran generative
AI models locally to see how far they would go. I enjoy designing a
solution as much as building the real thing, and that's where the maker
side comes from.

This site was born from a comment on LinkedIn. Someone asked where I kept
all these experiments, and the honest answer was "nowhere, it's all over
the place". So I decided to bring everything together in a space of my
own, away from the timelines, with notes on infrastructure, AI, automation
and weekend inventions. Since it's a digital garden, notes are never
finished: I go back to them and fix or complete them when I learn
something new.

The notes are written in Portuguese first and then translated into
English. The language link at the top of each page takes you to the other
version when there is one.

My full résumé, with roles, certifications and technologies, is on
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jansouza/). This is where I keep
what doesn't fit there.
